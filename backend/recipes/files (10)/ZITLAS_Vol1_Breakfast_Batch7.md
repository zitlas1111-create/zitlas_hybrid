## ZITLAS-REC-0301 — ZITLAS Microwave Moong Cheela Mug
Moong dal batter cooked directly in a microwave-safe mug — no tawa or gas needed at all, genuinely built for a hostel room with only a microwave.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, Muscle Gain | Diet: Vegan
Servings: 1 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 3 min | Total: 13 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Microwave

**Ingredients**
- Moong dal, soaked and ground — 50 g
- Onion, finely chopped — 20 g
- Water — 20 ml
- Oil — 3 ml
- Cumin seeds, turmeric — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Grind soaked moong dal with water into a thick batter; mix in onion, cumin seeds and turmeric.
2. Grease a microwave-safe mug with the oil and pour in the batter, filling only halfway.
3. Microwave on high for 2-3 minutes until set and cooked through (check with a toothpick).
4. Run a knife around the edge, tip out onto a plate, and eat.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~208 kcal | Protein ~12.2 g | Carbs ~31.3 g | Fat ~3.6 g | Fiber ~8.3 g
**Primary protein sources:** Moong dal
**Why it works:** No tawa, no gas, no induction — genuinely works with just a microwave and a mug; Same protein base as a regular cheela, just a different cooking vessel; Minimal cleanup: one mug, one bowl
**Tags:** Hostel Friendly, Student Friendly, No Cook, Quick Meal | Hostel Friendly: Yes | ZITLAS Original: Yes

## ZITLAS-REC-0303 — ZITLAS Microwave Oats Upma Mug
Savoury oats upma cooked entirely in a microwave-safe mug — a stove-free version of a familiar tempered dish.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan

**Ingredients**
- Rolled oats — 40 g
- Mixed vegetables, finely chopped — 30 g
- Water — 100 ml

## ZITLAS-REC-0312 — ZITLAS Microwave Poached Egg Mug

## ZITLAS-REC-0315 — ZITLAS Microwave Savoury Steamed Egg Mug
A silky, custard-like savoury steamed egg made in the microwave with milk and light seasoning — a distinctly different egg texture from an omelette or scramble.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain | Diet: Eggetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 5 min | Cook: 3 min | Total: 8 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Microwave

**Ingredients**
- Whole egg — 100 g
- Milk — 60 ml
- Spring onion, chopped — 10 g
- Soy sauce — 3 ml

**Instructions**
1. Whisk the egg with milk and soy sauce until smooth (avoid over-beating to reduce bubbles).
2. Pour into a microwave-safe mug.
3. Microwave on medium power for 2-3 minutes, checking every 45 seconds, until just set into a soft, custard-like texture.
4. Top with spring onion and eat with a spoon.

**Primary protein sources:** Whole egg, Milk
**Why it works:** Custard-like, silky texture is genuinely different from a fried or scrambled egg; Milk dilution and gentle microwave power give it a smoother mouthfeel than a regular mug omelette; Distinct East-Asian-inspired technique (chawanmushi-style) rarely seen in Indian fitness recipes

Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 5 min | Cook: 0 min | Total: 5 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: No-cook

**Ingredients**
- Cucumber, grated — 60 g
- Curd — 150 g
- Roasted chana (dry) — 20 g
- Roasted cumin powder — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out a little excess water.
2. Mix into curd along with roasted cumin powder.
3. Top with roasted chana just before eating for crunch.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~138 kcal | Protein ~7.9 g | Carbs ~15.5 g | Fat ~5.6 g | Fiber ~2.7 g
**Primary protein sources:** Curd, Roasted chana
**Why it works:** Roasted chana on top adds crunch and protein a plain raita wouldn't have; Genuinely zero cooking, very light; Cooling option for hot mornings

## ZITLAS-REC-0346 — ZITLAS Rice Cooker Moong Dal Khichdi
A softer, more porridge-like moong dal khichdi cooked with extra water in a rice cooker — a wetter consistency than the standard Rice Cooker Vegetable Khichdi in this batch, closer to a congee.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan
Servings: 1 | Prep: 8 min | Cook: 30 min | Total: 38 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Rice cooker

**Ingredients**
- Rice — 40 g
- Moong dal (dry) — 35 g
- Water — 250 ml
- Ghee — 5 g
- Cumin seeds — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Rinse rice and moong dal together.
2. Add to the rice cooker with a generous amount of water (deliberately more than a standard khichdi) and cumin seeds.
3. Run the cooking cycle, and if needed, run it a second time for a softer, more porridge-like result.
4. Stir in ghee before serving.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~214 kcal | Protein ~9.4 g | Carbs ~30.6 g | Fat ~5.8 g | Fiber ~6.3 g
**Primary protein sources:** Moong dal
**Why it works:** Deliberately wetter, congee-like consistency is a genuinely different texture from the firmer vegetable khichdi earlier in this batch; Very easy to digest, good for a light or unwell morning; Completely hands-off in the rice cooker

## ZITLAS-REC-0348 — ZITLAS Rice Cooker Savoury Rice Porridge
A Chinese-style savoury rice congee cooked in a rice cooker with a very high water ratio — a distinctly different rice preparation from every pulao, khichdi or biryani in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan
Servings: 1 | Prep: 5 min | Cook: 35 min | Total: 40 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Rice cooker

**Ingredients**
- Rice — 40 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Ginger, chopped — 5 g
- Spring onion, soy sauce — 5 g

**Instructions**
1. Add rice, water and chopped ginger to the rice cooker.
2. Run the normal cooking cycle, then run a second cycle if the rice hasn't broken down into a thick, porridge-like consistency.
3. Season with soy sauce and top with spring onion before serving.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~48 kcal | Protein ~1.0 g | Carbs ~10.0 g | Fat ~0.4 g | Fiber ~0.7 g
**Primary protein sources:** None (not a protein-focused recipe)
**Why it works:** Congee-style deliberately breaks the rice down into a smooth porridge, a texture and technique entirely unlike any pulao or khichdi in the database; Very gentle on the stomach; East-Asian technique applied with fully Indian-available ingredients
