## ZITLAS-REC-0351 — ZITLAS Odia Dalma Bowl
An Odisha-style dal cooked with mixed vegetables and a light panch phoron tempering — a distinct regional dal-vegetable combination not resembling any other dal dish in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: General Fitness, Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan | Region: Odia-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 20 min | Total: 30 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Pressure cooker

**Ingredients**
- Toor dal (dry) — 40 g
- Mixed vegetables (pumpkin, raw banana, papaya) — 70 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Panch phoron, turmeric — 4 g

**Instructions**
1. Pressure cook toor dal with mixed vegetables and turmeric for 3 whistles until soft.
2. Mash lightly.
3. Heat oil, temper panch phoron, and pour over the dal.
4. Simmer 3-4 minutes and serve hot.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~205 kcal | Protein ~10.0 g | Carbs ~26.3 g | Fat ~6.7 g | Fiber ~7.4 g
**Primary protein sources:** Toor dal
**Why it works:** Panch phoron tempering and the specific vegetable mix (pumpkin, raw banana) give this a genuinely distinct Odia flavour from any Punjabi or South Indian dal in the database; One-pot, mostly hands-off; Traditionally a lunch dish, reframed here as a hearty breakfast bowl
**Tags:** One Pot | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0352 — ZITLAS Kashmiri Nadru Yakhni Bowl
Lotus stem (nadru) simmered in a mild yogurt-based Kashmiri gravy — introducing lotus stem, a vegetable not used anywhere else in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Kashmiri-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 20 min | Total: 35 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Premium | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Lotus stem (nadru), sliced — 100 g
- Curd — 100 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Fennel powder, ginger powder, cardamom — 5 g

**Instructions**
1. Shallow-fry the sliced lotus stem in oil until lightly browned; set aside.
2. Whisk curd with fennel powder, ginger powder and cardamom.
3. Heat the whisked curd gently in a pan, stirring continuously to prevent curdling, until it thickens slightly.
4. Add the fried lotus stem and simmer 5-6 minutes.
5. Serve warm.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~138 kcal | Protein ~5.0 g | Carbs ~9.7 g | Fat ~9.5 g | Fiber ~2.0 g
**Primary protein sources:** Curd
**Why it works:** Lotus stem is a genuinely unique vegetable for the database, with a distinct crunchy-then-tender texture; Kashmiri yakhni technique (yogurt-based, no onion-garlic) is a distinct regional gravy style; Curd base supplies real protein

## ZITLAS-REC-0353 — ZITLAS Himachali Siddu
A steamed, stuffed whole wheat bread from Himachal Pradesh, filled with a spiced walnut-poppy seed paste — a distinct steamed-bread format not resembling any dhokla, idli or momo in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: General Fitness | Diet: Vegan | Region: Himachali-inspired
Servings: 2 | Prep: 90 min | Cook: 20 min | Total: 110 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Pressure cooker

**Ingredients**
- Whole wheat atta — 120 g
- Walnuts, crushed — 30 g
- Yeast — 2 g
- Oil — 8 ml
- Cumin seeds, salt — 3 g

**Instructions**
1. Knead atta with yeast and warm water into a soft dough; let it rise for about 1.5 hours until doubled.
2. Mix crushed walnuts with cumin seeds and a little oil for the filling.
3. Divide the dough, flatten each portion, add filling, and seal into a ball.
4. Steam in a pressure cooker (without the whistle) for 18-20 minutes until cooked through.
5. Serve with a drizzle of ghee or chutney.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~675 kcal | Protein ~18.9 g | Carbs ~88.2 g | Fat ~29.5 g | Fiber ~15.4 g
**Primary protein sources:** Walnuts
**Why it works:** Yeasted, steamed bread technique is entirely different from any dhokla, idli, khaman or momo already in the database; Walnut filling introduces a nut rarely used as a main filling elsewhere; Himachal Pradesh regional cuisine represented for the first time
**Tags:**  | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0354 — ZITLAS Chhattisgarhi Bore Basi
Cooked rice soaked overnight in water and served with curd and green chilli the next morning — a traditional fermented-rice breakfast distinct from any curd rice or poha dish in the database.

**Ingredients**
- Cooked rice — 150 g
- Water (for soaking) — 100 ml
- Curd — 60 g
- Green chilli, onion, salt — 6 g

**Instructions**

## ZITLAS-REC-0355 — ZITLAS Litti-Style Baked Sattu Balls
Whole wheat dough balls stuffed with spiced sattu and baked until crisp — the traditional Bihar-Jharkhand litti, made oven-baked instead of over open coals.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Vegan | Region: Bihar-Jharkhand-inspired
Servings: 2 | Prep: 20 min | Cook: 25 min | Total: 45 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Oven

**Ingredients**
- Sattu (roasted gram flour) — 80 g
- Whole wheat atta — 100 g
- Mustard oil — 10 ml
- Ajwain, nigella seeds, pickle masala — 6 g

**Instructions**
1. Mix sattu with mustard oil, ajwain, nigella seeds and pickle masala, adding a little water to form a moist, crumbly filling.
2. Knead atta into a firm dough and divide into balls; flatten each, stuff with the sattu filling and seal into a tight ball.
3. Bake at 200°C for 22-25 minutes, turning occasionally, until the crust is browned and firm.
4. Serve with a drizzle of ghee and a side of chokha or curd.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~732 kcal | Protein ~28.0 g | Carbs ~115.6 g | Fat ~16.5 g | Fiber ~19.2 g
**Primary protein sources:** Sattu
**Why it works:** Litti's stuffed-and-baked ball technique is distinct from the flat stuffed parathas already using sattu in the database; Baking is a genuine practical adaptation of the traditional coal-roasted method for a home kitchen; High protein, very low cost

## ZITLAS-REC-0361 — ZITLAS Office Lunchbox Rajma Rice Bowl
