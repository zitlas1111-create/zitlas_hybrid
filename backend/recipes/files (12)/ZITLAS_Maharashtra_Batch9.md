## ZITLAS-REC-0401 — ZITLAS Zunka Bhakri Bowl [Breakfast]
Dry, crumbly besan zunka served with jowar bhakri — a Maharashtrian staple combination reframed as a protein-and-fiber breakfast bowl.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegan | Region: Maharashtrian-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 25 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Tawa, Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Besan — 50 g
- Jowar flour — 60 g
- Onion, chopped — 40 g
- Oil — 8 ml
- Mustard seeds, turmeric, green chilli — 4 g

**Instructions**
1. Knead jowar flour with warm water and pat into a bhakri; cook on a hot tawa until browned on both sides.
2. Heat oil, temper mustard seeds, saute onion and green chilli until soft.
3. Sprinkle in besan gradually while stirring continuously to avoid lumps, along with turmeric and a little water, cooking until dry and crumbly (this is zunka, not a wet gravy).
4. Serve the zunka hot alongside the bhakri.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~476 kcal | Protein ~17.7 g | Carbs ~76.2 g | Fat ~12.1 g | Fiber ~10.1 g
**Primary protein sources:** Besan
**Why it works:** Zunka's dry, crumbly texture is genuinely different from a wet besan gravy like kadhi or pithla; Jowar bhakri is naturally gluten-free and fiber-rich; Everyday Maharashtrian farmer's meal, genuinely practical
**Tags:** High Fiber, Budget Friendly | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0402 — ZITLAS Pithla Bhakri Bowl [Breakfast]
A wet, gravy-like besan pithla served with bhakri — distinct from the dry zunka by its looser, saucier consistency.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan | Region: Maharashtrian-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 8 min | Cook: 12 min | Total: 20 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Tawa, Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Besan — 50 g
- Jowar flour — 60 g
- Water — 120 ml
- Oil — 7 ml
- Garlic, cumin seeds, green chilli — 5 g

**Instructions**
1. Knead jowar flour with warm water and pat into a bhakri; cook on a hot tawa.

## ZITLAS-REC-0403 — ZITLAS Tarri Poha [Breakfast]
A Nagpur-style spicy poha topped with a thin, fiery tarri (gravy) — distinct from the standard kanda poha already in the database by the added spiced gravy layer.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan | Region: Nagpur-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 12 min | Total: 22 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Poha (flattened rice) — 50 g
- Onion, chopped — 40 g
- Tomato, chopped — 30 g
- Peanuts — 15 g
- Oil — 7 ml
- Nagpuri tarri masala (red chilli, garlic) — 5 g

**Instructions**
1. Rinse poha and set aside.
2. For the tarri, heat oil, saute half the onion and all the tomato with the fiery Nagpuri masala, adding water to form a thin, spicy gravy; simmer 8-10 minutes.
3. Separately temper the remaining onion and peanuts in a little oil, then toss with the poha as usual.
4. Serve the poha topped with a generous ladle of hot tarri.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~343 kcal | Protein ~8.0 g | Carbs ~45.7 g | Fat ~15.1 g | Fiber ~3.5 g

## ZITLAS-REC-0404 — ZITLAS Amboli [Breakfast]
A thick, fermented rice-and-urad pancake from the Konkan coast — spongier and thicker than a dosa, with a distinct fermented tang.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan | Region: Konkan-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 480 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 490 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Tawa, Mixer

**Ingredients**
- Rice, soaked — 60 g
- Urad dal, soaked — 20 g
- Cooked rice — 20 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Salt — 1 g

**Instructions**
1. Soak rice and urad dal separately for several hours, then grind together with a little cooked rice (this gives amboli its characteristic softness) into a thick batter.
2. Ferment overnight at room temperature.
3. Heat a tawa, pour a thick ladle of batter and spread only slightly — amboli stays thick, unlike a dosa.
4. Cover and cook until the top is set and full of small holes, then flip briefly.
5. Serve with coconut chutney.
6. Note: soak and overnight ferment folded into prep time above.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~365 kcal | Protein ~9.1 g | Carbs ~64.8 g | Fat ~7.4 g | Fiber ~5.4 g
**Primary protein sources:** Urad dal
**Why it works:** Thicker, spongier texture with a distinct fermented tang from Konkan coastal cooking, genuinely different from the thin, crisp dosas already in the database; Adding cooked rice to the batter is a specific regional technique that changes the final texture; Coastal Maharashtra rarely represented in fitness recipe collections

## ZITLAS-REC-0408 — ZITLAS Kothimbir Vadi Bowl [Breakfast]
Steamed besan-and-coriander vadi, sliced and lightly pan-tempered — a snack traditionally fried, made lighter here as a breakfast bowl.

**Ingredients**
- Besan — 60 g
- Coriander leaves, chopped — 30 g
- Water — 50 ml
- Oil — 6 ml
- Sesame seeds, turmeric, green chilli — 4 g

**Instructions**
