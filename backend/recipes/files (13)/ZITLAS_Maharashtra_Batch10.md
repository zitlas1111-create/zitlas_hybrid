## ZITLAS-REC-0461 — ZITLAS Sprouted Moong Sheera Bowl [Breakfast]
A savoury-sweet sheera made from ground sprouted moong instead of rava — a distinct grain-free take on the traditional semolina sheera already in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Maharashtrian-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 20 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Moong sprouts, ground — 70 g
- Milk — 100 ml
- Jaggery — 15 g
- Ghee — 6 g

**Instructions**
1. Heat ghee in a kadai, add ground moong sprouts and roast for 3-4 minutes.
2. Add milk gradually, stirring continuously.
3. Once thickened, stir in jaggery and cook 2 more minutes.
4. Serve warm.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~399 kcal | Protein ~20.4 g | Carbs ~57.3 g | Fat ~10.2 g | Fiber ~12.4 g
**Primary protein sources:** Moong sprouts
**Why it works:** Moong sprouts base gives notably more protein and fiber than the semolina used in a traditional sheera; Distinct grain-free composition from the Rava Sheera Protein Bowl already in the database; Naturally sweet, comforting texture

## ZITLAS-REC-0462 — ZITLAS Nachni Ambil Breakfast Bowl [Breakfast]
A fermented ragi (nachni) porridge mixed with curd — a ragi-based variant of the bajra Dahi Ambil already in the database, offering a genuinely different grain and mineral profile.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Maharashtrian-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 600 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 610 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Ragi flour — 40 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Curd — 100 g
- Roasted cumin powder, black salt — 2 g

**Instructions**

## ZITLAS-REC-0465 — ZITLAS Moong Amboli [Breakfast]
Amboli made with ground moong dal instead of urad dal — a protein-boosted, genuinely different legume base from the classic Konkan amboli already in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, Weight Loss | Diet: Vegan | Region: Konkan-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 480 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 490 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Tawa, Mixer

**Ingredients**
- Rice, soaked — 60 g
- Moong dal, soaked — 30 g
- Cooked rice — 20 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Salt — 1 g

**Instructions**
1. Soak rice and moong dal separately, then grind together with a little cooked rice into a thick batter.
2. Ferment for 6-8 hours.
3. Heat a tawa, pour a thick ladle of batter and spread only slightly.
4. Cover and cook until the top sets with small holes, then flip briefly.
5. Serve with chutney.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~401 kcal | Protein ~11.3 g | Carbs ~70.7 g | Fat ~7.4 g | Fiber ~6.6 g
**Primary protein sources:** Moong dal
**Why it works:** Moong dal ferments and cooks differently from urad dal, giving a genuinely different flavour and slightly higher protein than the classic amboli already in the database; Same thick, spongy Konkan technique, different legume; Good protein-forward alternative for users who prefer moong over urad
