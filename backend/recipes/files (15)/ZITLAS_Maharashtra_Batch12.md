## ZITLAS-REC-0584 — ZITLAS Kulith Pithi Bhakri [Breakfast]

**Ingredients**
- Horse gram (kulith) flour — 70 g

## ZITLAS-REC-0585 — ZITLAS Gavar Bhaji with Bhakri [Breakfast]

- Gavar (cluster beans), chopped — 120 g

## ZITLAS-REC-0587 — ZITLAS Ukad Rice Dumpling Bowl [Breakfast]

**Ingredients**
- Rice flour — 60 g
- Boiling water — 50 ml
- Onion, chopped — 25 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Mustard seeds, turmeric — 3 g

**Instructions**
1. Knead rice flour with boiling water into a smooth dough, then shape into small irregular dumplings by hand (no pleating or precise shaping needed).
