## ZITLAS-REC-0001
**ZITLAS Protein Crunch Upma**
Description: Upma rebuilt with a doubled protein load from {p1} and {p2} while keeping the familiar flavour.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain | Diet: Vegetarian
Servings: 1 | Time: 20 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Kadai

Ingredients:
- Rava — 40 g
- Soya chunks (rehydrated, chopped) — 30 g
- Curd/Greek yogurt — 100 g
- Onion — 30 g, chopped
- Capsicum — 30 g, chopped
- Oil — 5 ml
- Mustard seeds / cumin — 1/4 tsp
- Salt — to taste
- Green chilli — 1, chopped (optional)

Instructions:
1. Dry-roast or rinse rava as needed and keep aside.
2. Heat oil in a kadai, add mustard seeds/cumin and let them splutter.
3. Add Onion and Capsicum, cook 3-4 minutes until softened.
4. Fold in soya chunks (rehydrated, chopped) and curd/greek yogurt in the last two minutes so they stay tender, not mushy.
5. Add the rava with a splash of water/milk as needed, mix, and cook covered until done.
6. Season with salt and chilli, mix well, and serve hot.

Nutrition/serving (estimated): Calories ~260 kcal | Protein ~25 g | Carbs ~35 g | Fat ~9 g | Fiber ~4 g
Primary protein sources: Soya chunks (rehydrated, chopped), Curd/Greek yogurt
Why it works: High protein for a breakfast meal; Vegetables add fiber and micronutrient variety; Practical for everyday preparation
Tags: Protein-Upgrade, High Protein
