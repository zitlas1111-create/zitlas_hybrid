## ZITLAS-REC-0001 — ZITLAS Sattu Power Shot
A 2-minute Bihar-inspired no-cook drink-meal made by whisking roasted gram flour into water with lemon and spices — built for a hostel room with zero cooking equipment.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegan | Region: Bihar-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 2 min | Cook: 0 min | Total: 2 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: No-cook

**Ingredients**
- Sattu (roasted gram flour) — 40 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Roasted cumin powder — 2 g
- Black salt — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Add sattu to a glass or bottle.
2. Pour in water and whisk hard with a spoon or fork until no lumps remain.
3. Stir in lemon juice, roasted cumin powder and black salt.
4. Drink immediately — sattu settles if left standing.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~152 kcal | Protein ~8.0 g | Carbs ~22.8 g | Fat ~2.4 g | Fiber ~4.0 g
**Primary protein sources:** Sattu (roasted gram flour)
**Why it works:** High protein with zero cooking; Works with just a bottle and spoon; Very low cost

## ZITLAS-REC-0002 — ZITLAS Moong Cheela Paneer Roll
A savoury moong dal crepe rolled around a lightly spiced paneer-spinach filling — a complete protein breakfast eaten like a wrap.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, Body Transformation | Diet: Vegetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 25 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Tawa, Mixer

**Ingredients**
- Moong dal (soaked, ground to batter) — 50 g
- Paneer, crumbled — 50 g
- Spinach, chopped — 30 g
- Onion, chopped — 20 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Ginger-green chilli paste — 5 g
- Cumin seeds — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Grind soaked moong dal with ginger-chilli paste and a little water into a smooth, pourable batter.
2. Heat a tawa, pour a ladle of batter and spread into a thin crepe. Cook 2 minutes until set.
3. Meanwhile, saute onion in oil with cumin seeds, then add paneer and spinach; cook 3-4 minutes.
4. Place the filling along one edge of the cheela and roll it up tightly.
5. Slice in half and serve warm.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~374 kcal | Protein ~22.1 g | Carbs ~33.0 g | Fat ~16.7 g | Fiber ~9.0 g
**Primary protein sources:** Moong dal, Paneer
**Why it works:** Two complementary protein sources in one wrap; Naturally gluten-free; Portable once rolled
**Tags:** High Protein, Home Friendly, High Fiber | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0003 — ZITLAS Leftover Rice Protein Poha
Turns yesterday's plain cooked rice into a soft, savoury breakfast by pan-tossing it poha-style with peanuts, moong sprouts and turmeric — no fresh grain needed.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegan | Region: Maharashtrian-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 5 min | Cook: 8 min | Total: 13 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Cooked leftover rice (cold) — 150 g
- Moong sprouts — 40 g
- Peanuts — 15 g
- Onion, chopped — 30 g
- Oil — 5 ml
- Mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric — 3 g

**Instructions**
1. Heat oil in a kadai, add mustard seeds and let them splutter, then add curry leaves and peanuts, roast lightly.
2. Add onion and saute until translucent.
3. Add moong sprouts and cook 2-3 minutes.
4. Add cold leftover rice and turmeric, breaking up any clumps, and toss on medium heat for 3-4 minutes until heated through.
5. Serve hot with a squeeze of lemon.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~460 kcal | Protein ~17.7 g | Carbs ~66.2 g | Fat ~14.4 g | Fiber ~10.9 g
**Primary protein sources:** Moong sprouts, Peanuts
**Why it works:** Prevents food waste from last night's rice; Sprouts add fresh crunch and protein; Ready in under 15 minutes
**Tags:** Budget Friendly, Quick Meal, One Pan | Hostel Friendly: Yes | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0007 — ZITLAS Besan Dhokla Bites
Steamed gram-flour dhokla made in an idli mould, giving the classic Gujarati texture without deep steamer equipment.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Gujarati-inspired
Servings: 2 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 25 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Pressure cooker

**Ingredients**
- Besan — 100 g
- Curd — 50 g
- Water — 60 ml
- Eno fruit salt / baking soda — 3 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Mustard seeds, sesame seeds — 4 g

**Instructions**
1. Whisk besan, curd and water into a smooth, thick-pourable batter.
2. Just before steaming, mix in the fruit salt/baking soda — it will froth slightly.
3. Pour into a greased idli mould or shallow tin and steam in a pressure cooker (without the whistle) for 12-15 minutes.
4. Cool slightly, cut into squares.
5. Temper oil with mustard and sesame seeds and pour over the top.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~443 kcal | Protein ~23.8 g | Carbs ~60.4 g | Fat ~13.7 g | Fiber ~10.8 g
**Primary protein sources:** Besan
**Why it works:** Steamed, not fried — very low added fat; Uses standard home pressure cooker as a steamer; Good fiber from besan
**Tags:** Home Friendly, High Fiber | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0010 — ZITLAS Curd Rice with Roasted Peanuts

- Curd — 100 g
- Peanuts — 15 g
- Milk — 20 ml
- Oil — 4 ml

Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, Body Transformation | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Punjabi-inspired

A fibre-forward finger-millet dosa batter pan-cooked thin and crisp, served with a quick tomato chutney — a fiber-focused twist on a South Indian classic.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegan | Region: South Indian-inspired
