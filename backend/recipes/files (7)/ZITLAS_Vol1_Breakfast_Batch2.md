## ZITLAS-REC-0051 — ZITLAS Masala Oats Khichdi
Rolled oats cooked khichdi-style with toor dal and vegetables in one pot — a savoury oats format for people who dislike sweet porridge oats.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegan
Servings: 1 | Prep: 8 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 23 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Pressure cooker

**Ingredients**
- Rolled oats — 40 g
- Toor dal (dry) — 25 g
- Mixed vegetables, chopped — 50 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Mustard seeds, turmeric, curry leaves — 3 g

**Instructions**
1. Rinse toor dal and pressure cook with vegetables and turmeric for 2 whistles until soft.
2. Dry-roast the oats separately for 2 minutes until lightly toasted.
3. Heat oil, temper mustard seeds and curry leaves, add the cooked dal-vegetable mix and the roasted oats with a little water.
4. Simmer 3-4 minutes until the oats soften into a khichdi consistency.
5. Serve hot.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~301 kcal | Protein ~11.5 g | Carbs ~43.5 g | Fat ~9.2 g | Fiber ~8.8 g
**Primary protein sources:** Toor dal
**Why it works:** Savoury format avoids the usual sweet-oats fatigue; One-pot, mostly hands-off cooking; Dal brings meaningfully more protein than oats alone
**Tags:** One Pot, Budget Friendly | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0052 — ZITLAS Besan Paneer Roll
A besan crepe rolled (not folded) around a dry-spiced paneer filling — a different construction from a folded chilla, closer to a kathi roll.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 12 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 22 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Tawa

**Ingredients**
- Besan — 50 g
- Water — 70 ml
- Paneer, crumbled — 50 g
- Onion, thinly sliced — 25 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Chaat masala — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Whisk besan and water into a thin, dosa-like batter.
2. Pan-cook a thin round on a hot tawa, similar to a dosa rather than a thick chilla.
3. Separately dry-toss paneer with onion and chaat masala (no extra oil needed here).
4. Lay the filling along one edge of the thin besan crepe and roll tightly kathi-roll style.
5. Slice and serve.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~376 kcal | Protein ~20.3 g | Carbs ~31.9 g | Fat ~19.0 g | Fiber ~5.8 g
**Primary protein sources:** Besan, Paneer
**Why it works:** Thin-roll format is a genuinely different eating experience from a folded chilla; Dry-tossed filling keeps added fat lower; Portable once rolled
**Tags:** High Protein, Quick Meal | Hostel Friendly: Yes | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0053 — ZITLAS Egg Curry with Multigrain Roti
Boiled eggs simmered in a light onion-tomato curry, served with a multigrain roti — a curry format rather than the usual bhurji or omelette.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Eggetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 25 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Kadai, Tawa

**Ingredients**
- Boiled egg — 100 g
- Onion, chopped — 40 g
- Tomato, chopped — 40 g
- Multigrain atta — 60 g
- Oil — 8 ml
- Ginger-garlic paste, garam masala — 5 g

**Instructions**
1. Knead the multigrain atta into a dough and roll into rotis; roast on a tawa and keep warm.
2. Heat oil, saute onion until golden, add ginger-garlic paste and tomato, cook until pulpy.
3. Add garam masala and a little water to form a light gravy; simmer 5 minutes.
4. Halve the boiled eggs and add them into the gravy, simmering 2-3 minutes.
5. Serve hot with the rotis.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~453 kcal | Protein ~19.8 g | Carbs ~47.1 g | Fat ~20.9 g | Fiber ~7.2 g
**Primary protein sources:** Egg
**Why it works:** Curry format is a genuinely different way to eat eggs from bhurji or omelette; Multigrain roti adds fiber the usual white-flour roti lacks; Filling, complete meal-style breakfast
**Tags:** Home Friendly | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0058 — ZITLAS Egg-Coated Paratha
A whole wheat paratha dipped in beaten egg and pan-cooked so the egg forms a coating on the outside, rather than being stuffed inside — an anda-paratha street-food style.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Eggetarian | Region: North Indian street-food-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 8 min | Cook: 8 min | Total: 16 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Tawa

**Ingredients**
- Whole wheat atta — 60 g
- Whole egg — 100 g
- Onion, finely chopped — 20 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Black pepper, chilli flakes — 2 g

**Instructions**
1. Knead atta into a dough, roll out and dry-roast a plain paratha until about 70% done.

3. Dip the half-cooked paratha into the beaten egg, coating one side.

## ZITLAS-REC-0062 — ZITLAS Moong Dal Appe
A moong dal and semolina batter steamed-fried into small round appe (paniyaram) using a dedicated appe pan — a distinctly different cooking format from a flat crepe.

## ZITLAS-REC-0063 — ZITLAS Oats Besan Dhokla
A steamed dhokla batter made with ground oats mixed into besan — a fiber-boosted variation on the classic Gujarati steamed snack.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Weight Loss, General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Gujarati-inspired
Servings: 2 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 30 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Pressure cooker, Mixer

**Ingredients**
- Besan — 60 g
- Rolled oats, ground — 40 g
- Curd — 40 g
- Water — 50 ml
- Eno fruit salt — 3 g
- Oil — 6 ml
- Mustard seeds, sesame seeds — 4 g

**Instructions**
1. Whisk besan, ground oats, curd and water into a smooth, thick-pourable batter.

## ZITLAS-REC-0066 — ZITLAS Roasted Chana Ladoo Bites
No-bake bites made from roasted chana flour, jaggery and a little ghee — a besan-free alternative to the peanut chikki bites, portable for a rushed morning.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian
Servings: 4 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 8 min | Total: 23 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Roasted chana flour (bhuna chana powder) — 100 g
- Jaggery — 50 g
- Ghee — 15 g
