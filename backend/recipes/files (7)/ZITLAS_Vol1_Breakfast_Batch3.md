## ZITLAS-REC-0101 — ZITLAS Paneer Tikka Skewers
Paneer cubes marinated in curd and spices, grilled on skewers — a tikka format eaten as breakfast rather than an evening starter.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, Body Transformation | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Punjabi-inspired
Servings: 1 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 12 min | Total: 27 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Oven, Air fryer

**Ingredients**
- Paneer, cubed — 100 g
- Curd — 40 g
- Capsicum, cubed — 30 g
- Onion, cubed — 30 g
- Oil — 5 ml
- Tikka masala — 4 g

**Instructions**
1. Marinate paneer, capsicum and onion cubes in curd and tikka masala for at least 15 minutes.
2. Thread onto skewers, alternating paneer and vegetables.
3. Grill in an oven or air fryer at 200°C for 10-12 minutes, turning once, until lightly charred.
4. Serve hot.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~353 kcal | Protein ~20.2 g | Carbs ~7.3 g | Fat ~26.4 g | Fiber ~1.1 g
**Primary protein sources:** Paneer, Curd
**Why it works:** Grilled, not fried — very little added oil; Skewer format is genuinely different from any bhurji, roll or toast in the database; High protein for a light breakfast
**Tags:** High Protein | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: Yes

## ZITLAS-REC-0103 — ZITLAS Baked Vegetable Frittata Slice
A single large egg-and-vegetable frittata baked in one pan and cut into slices — different from individual muffin cups, meant to be sliced and shared or portioned.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Eggetarian
Servings: 3 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 25 min | Total: 40 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Oven

**Ingredients**
- Whole egg — 250 g
- Onion, chopped — 50 g
- Capsicum, chopped — 50 g
- Spinach, chopped — 40 g
- Oil — 8 ml
- Black pepper, salt — 3 g

**Instructions**
1. Preheat oven to 180°C and grease a round baking dish.
2. Saute onion, capsicum and spinach in oil for 3-4 minutes until slightly softened.
3. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper, then pour over the vegetables in the greased dish.
4. Bake for 20-25 minutes until fully set and lightly golden on top.
5. Cool slightly, slice like a pie, and serve.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~500 kcal | Protein ~35.0 g | Carbs ~11.2 g | Fat ~35.8 g | Fiber ~2.7 g
**Primary protein sources:** Whole egg
**Why it works:** One large bake serves multiple people, unlike single-portion muffin cups; Slices reheat well for a couple of days; Good option for a shared family breakfast
**Tags:** Family Friendly, Meal Prep | Hostel Friendly: No | ZITLAS Original: No

## ZITLAS-REC-0106 — ZITLAS Roasted Flaxseed Peanut Bites
No-bake bites made from roasted flaxseed powder and peanuts bound with jaggery — a different seed base from the chikki or ladoo bites elsewhere, adding an omega-3 angle.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: General Fitness | Diet: Vegan
Servings: 4 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 8 min | Total: 23 min | Difficulty: Easy | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Kadai

**Ingredients**
- Flaxseed, roasted and ground — 40 g
- Peanuts, roasted and crushed — 80 g
- Jaggery — 50 g
- Ghee — 10 g

**Instructions**
1. Melt jaggery with ghee in a kadai on low heat until syrupy.
2. Turn off heat, quickly mix in ground flaxseed and crushed peanuts, coating evenly.
3. Shape into 8 small balls while still warm.
4. Cool completely and store in an airtight container for up to a week.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~898 kcal | Protein ~29.1 g | Carbs ~61.9 g | Fat ~66.2 g | Fiber ~21.6 g

## ZITLAS-REC-0107 — ZITLAS Paneer Stuffed Naan Bites
Small naan breads stuffed with spiced paneer and cooked in a pan — a naan-based format, distinct from roti, kulcha or paratha stuffing elsewhere in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian | Region: Punjabi-inspired
Servings: 2 | Prep: 20 min | Cook: 15 min | Total: 35 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Tawa

**Ingredients**

- Curd (for dough) — 30 g
- Paneer, crumbled — 60 g
- Onion, finely chopped — 20 g
- Oil — 8 ml
- Baking soda, chaat masala — 3 g
