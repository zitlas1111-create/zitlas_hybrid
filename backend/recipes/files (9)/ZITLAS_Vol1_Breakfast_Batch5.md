## ZITLAS-REC-0201 — ZITLAS Oats Waffle with Peanut Butter
A crisp waffle made from an oats-egg batter, topped with peanut butter — a waffle format not used anywhere else in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: Muscle Gain, General Fitness | Diet: Eggetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 8 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: 18 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Moderate | Equipment: Tawa

**Ingredients**
- Rolled oats, ground — 40 g
- Whole egg — 50 g
- Milk — 40 ml
- Peanut butter — 15 g

**Instructions**
1. Blend ground oats, egg and milk into a smooth batter.
2. Cook on a hot, lightly greased tawa in a thick round, pressing lightly for a waffle-like crisp edge, 2-3 minutes per side.
3. Top with peanut butter and serve.

**Nutrition/serving (estimated):** Calories ~342 kcal | Protein ~16.7 g | Carbs ~32.3 g | Fat ~17.1 g | Fiber ~4.9 g
**Primary protein sources:** Whole egg, Peanut butter
**Why it works:** Waffle-style crisp texture is genuinely new to the database; No refined flour or added sugar; Quick, one-pan

## ZITLAS-REC-0203 — ZITLAS Chicken Tikka Wrap
Curd-marinated grilled chicken chunks rolled inside a roti — a non-vegetarian tikka format paralleling the Paneer Tikka Skewers but in wrap form.

**Ingredients**
- Chicken breast, cubed — 100 g
- Curd (marinade) — 30 g
- Whole wheat atta — 70 g

## ZITLAS-REC-0207 — ZITLAS Soya Seekh Kebab
Minced, spiced soya granules shaped onto skewers and pan-seared — a skewered kebab format distinct from the tikka-cube or loose-filling soya recipes elsewhere.

- Onion, finely chopped — 25 g

## ZITLAS-REC-0212 — ZITLAS Vegetable Idli Manchurian

## ZITLAS-REC-0221 — ZITLAS Baingan Paneer Paratha
A stuffed paratha filled with roasted mashed eggplant (baingan) mixed with paneer — a vegetable-and-protein filling combination not used elsewhere in the database.
Meal: Breakfast | Goals: General Fitness | Diet: Vegetarian
Servings: 1 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 12 min | Total: 27 min | Difficulty: Moderate | Cost: Budget | Equipment: Tawa

**Ingredients**
- Eggplant (baingan), roasted and mashed — 60 g
- Paneer, crumbled — 40 g
- Whole wheat atta — 70 g
- Oil — 8 ml
- Cumin powder, chilli powder — 3 g

**Instructions**
1. Roast the eggplant directly over a flame or in the oven until soft, then peel and mash.
2. Mix with crumbled paneer, cumin powder and chilli powder.
